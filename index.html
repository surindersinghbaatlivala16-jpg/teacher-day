01 · ABOUT TEACHERS' DAY

What is Teachers' Day?

Teachers' Day is celebrated in India every year on 5 September to honour the dedication, hard work and contribution of teachers.

The day also marks the birth anniversary of Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, a renowned philosopher, scholar, educationist and former President of India.

Teachers' Day gives students an opportunity to express gratitude to educators who have helped them learn, grow and become responsible individuals.

Teachers' Day 2026

02 · HISTORY

Why is Teachers' Day celebrated on 5 September?

1888

Birth of Radhakrishnan

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan was born on 5 September 1888 in Tiruttani, Tamil Nadu.

1962

The Beginning

When some of his students wanted to celebrate his birthday, he suggested that the day should instead be dedicated to teachers.

5 SEPT

Teachers' Day in India

Since then, India has observed 5 September as Teachers' Day to recognize the invaluable contribution of educators.

THE MAN BEHIND THE DAY

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan was an influential Indian philosopher, teacher, educationist, author and statesman.

He made significant contributions to Indian education and philosophy and strongly believed in the power of education and teachers.

1888 Born
1952 Vice President
1962 President
1954 Bharat Ratna

Major Contributions

  • Promoted higher education.
  • Contributed to Indian philosophy.
  • Worked as an educator and scholar.
  • Inspired generations of students.
  • Supported the importance of teachers in nation building.
